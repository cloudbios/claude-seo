# Synthesis for a sampled SEO audit

Use the retained report snapshot and per-page evidence. Recommendations follow
PERCEIVE → ANALYZE → VALIDATE → ACT; severity is an output of that reasoning,
not a substitute for it. These phases adapt upstream
`skills/seo/references/thinking-framework.md` and
`thinking-framework-validate-act.md` under the included MIT license. They do
not activate upstream provider, rendering, rewriting, baseline or scheduling
workflows. Content, images, local and hreflang reviews remain limited to the
assigned captured source and retained transport metadata.

## Perceive

State what was actually inspected: explicit sample, capture representation,
source and capture hashes, successful and failed checks, completed/partial
roles and unresolved work. Separate supplied HTML from HTTP observations.
Read the user's question and existing page intent before proposing changes.
Infer business type only when supported by that sample; name the assumption.

## Analyze

Connect the selected roles' observations supported by the same capture.
For example, an observed robots directive and contradictory markup may need
one coordinated review. A missing rendered representation can explain apparent
missing content; do not turn that uncertainty into a confirmed absence.
Compare repeated metadata only within the supplied sample. An observation
about those pages does not establish site-wide duplication or orphan status.

For content, distinguish observed attribution, dates, sourcing and claimed
experience from verified facts, credentials or reputation. Use actual excerpts
to explain clarity, repetition and gaps relative to the user's intent. Do not
treat crude document word counts or total link counts as measures of quality
or citations, or infer AI authorship. For images, distinguish missing alt from
empty alt and possible decoration; declared sizing, responsive and loading
markup from visual accuracy, intrinsic dimensions and measured performance.
File extensions do not verify formats or bytes. Missing rendering cannot
establish above-fold placement, an LCP image or actual CLS.

For local findings, keep business identity, address, phone, hours and map or
review links tied to their specific on-page entity. Differences between a
person, department and branch are not necessarily business inconsistencies.
A map link does not verify a Business Profile, and a quoted rating does not
establish review health. Service-area businesses may intentionally omit a
public street address; do not prescribe publication without that context.

For hreflang, separate the page's declarations from unobserved alternate
pages. Missing return links, target availability, content equivalence and the
search engine's canonical selection remain unverified. No declarations on a
monolingual page need not be a defect. Do not amplify a single questionable
annotation into a claim that the whole site's hreflang is ignored.

Prefer the underlying, evidenced cause over multiple recommendations for its
symptoms. Separate observed markup from actual search-engine indexing, rich
results, rankings or measured performance; those are not established here.

## Validate

Check each proposal against page purpose, user constraints and available
evidence. State unresolved assumptions; avoid claimed ranking gains or numeric
impact without supporting measurement. Do not treat a preferred title length,
word count or presence of optional schema as an eligibility requirement.

For each recommendation, include:

- **Evidence:** page, exact artifact/field or excerpt, and relevant check state.
- **Uncertainty:** what the capture or available roles cannot establish.
- **Remedy and dependency:** a concrete proposed change or investigation, and
  what must be resolved first.
- **Falsification/check:** an observable result that would show the diagnosis
  or proposed remedy was wrong, or confirm the defect was addressed.
- **Next indicator:** what to inspect next; external measurements may be
  proposed for later authorized work, never described as already observed.

Prioritize Critical, High, Medium or Low only as justified by the observed
effect and context. Use Info for relevant observations without an established
defect. A failed audit check is not automatically a critical site defect, and
missing evidence is not evidence of a penalty.

## Act

Write `ACTION-PLAN.md` referencing the numbered report snapshot used. Group
related remedies and sequence dependencies; mark independent work where useful.
Keep recommendations distinct from actions already performed. Do not modify
the target site, generate replacement clinical/business claims, submit URLs,
create monitoring baselines or schedule tasks from this synthesis workflow.

End with what the next review should inspect and which unresolved question it
would answer. Preserve incomplete coverage and active-claim blockers even when
the available findings are useful. Do not present an overall SEO health score,
numeric content/readability or E-E-A-T scores from this sample. Name the roles
actually selected and reviewed so a focused report cannot imply six-role
coverage.
